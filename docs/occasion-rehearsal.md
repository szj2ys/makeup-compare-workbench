# Plan an occasion look with two references

Use this worksheet before a wedding, party or photo session. Start with the products you already own. You do not need an AI preview or a personal-photo upload.

## Describe the occasion

- Event and time of day: ______________________________
- Lighting to practise in: ______________________________
- Time available to get ready: _________________________
- The effect I want: ___________________________________

Choose two references that help answer one question, such as “Do I prefer a softer eye or a more defined lash line?” A different face, camera angle or lighting setup can change the comparison. An illustrative generated image cannot establish a foundation match or predict how a product will wear.

If you use the Makeup Comparison Workbench, put your question in **What are you comparing?** Choose the honest option under **Face, crop, and lighting**. Describe visible differences in **What can you actually see?**, and put the two details you want to practise in **One thing to try in real life**. You can type notes without uploading images; use only images you have permission to use if a visual comparison helps.

## Rehearse once

Try the two details when there is time to change your mind. Look in ordinary daylight and the indoor light you expect at the event. If photos matter, take a practice photo in your usual camera app and compare it with the mirror.

- The detail I want to keep: ____________________________
- The detail I want to soften or simplify: ________________
- What changes between the two lighting conditions: ______
- What I will skip if I have ten fewer minutes: ___________

## Keep the useful result

Pack the products you actually used and retain one final reference. The aim is a repeatable plan, not an exact imitation of another face.

The workbench's **Export notes** button saves your text, labels and image dimensions as JSON. It does not include your images or their original filenames. There is no automatic save, and this version cannot import an exported file. Keep the downloaded notes somewhere you can find them, or print this worksheet. Avoid putting private details in notes you intend to share.
